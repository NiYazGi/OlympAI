Scope: the signed-in app (Operate). Primary surface: a problem and its graded attempt; the shell, dashboard and lists follow the same system.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The protocol reads like a physics wall chart in a classroom: numbered callouts with a bracket on the student's own lines, a legend under them, and every colour meaning one thing on every screen. Refuses the ed-tech dashboard of rounded tiles, counters and empty radars.

OWN-WORLD: School-board green rail #1F3B33 with white labels and a chrome-yellow #F2B705 marker; white working plane split by 1px rules #CBD3CE, "given" material on wall tint #E8EEEA; ink #161A18, graphite #56605B. Three poster inks, one job each: cinnabar #C7351C error, chrome #F2B705 gap, cobalt #2346C4 strong idea, links, focus. Sofia Sans in three widths, no monospace; regions, not cards; radius 0 on regions, 4px on controls, circles only on callouts.

STORY: The student sees the points first, then exactly which lines carried or broke the argument, then one next step with its reason.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Rail left (240px). Header row: problem title, source line, code/max/time on the right. Below, statement on wall tint (5/12) beside the attempt (7/12): points out of 7 in condensed 800 type with the verdict, the summary, then numbered lines with callouts in a 56px right gutter. Primary action "Исправить решение" in ink under the legend.

FORM: Classroom wall charts (учебные таблицы), candidate 4 of 7 on the ordered list, assigned by the roll; seed key a18e1b80. Raises kept: closed set of three callout kinds with a permanent key; lists as dense tables; one primary command with its reason per screen; whole paper as one list; short stable problem code; only the changed cell moves.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
